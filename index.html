The problem

Sales can scale faster than the operation behind them.

A growing shop can hide costly patterns inside returns, refunds, customer complaints and fulfilment. Stonevale turns those patterns into a short list of actions.

01

Returns hide patterns

A headline refund number does not tell you which products, reasons or processes are creating the loss.

02

Teams become reactive

Fast growth can leave founders and small teams solving cases one-by-one instead of fixing repeat causes.

03

Shop health gets dragged down

Seller-fault issues, slow aftersales and poor customer signals can affect more than the individual refund.

Questions

What founders usually want to know.

Do you need my TikTok Shop password?

No. We prefer exports, screenshots or appropriately limited Seller Centre permissions. You should never hand over your owner password.

Are you trying to reject legitimate customer returns?

No. The goal is not to obstruct valid returns. We identify patterns behind avoidable seller-fault issues and operational causes while separating those from normal customer behaviour.

How quickly do I receive the audit?

Our target is five business days from receiving the complete data required for the review.

Is Stonevale a TikTok marketing agency?

No. Stonevale focuses on the operational side after sales start happening — refunds, returns, product issues, aftersales and shop-health signals.
