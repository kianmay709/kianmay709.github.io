A few useful answers

Before we talk.

Do you only work with ecommerce businesses?

No. Ecommerce, wholesale and product businesses are our primary focus. We also consider other sectors where the operational challenge fits our expertise and we can agree a realistic scope.

How are automation and custom software different?

Systems & Automation improves how existing tools work together. Custom Software Development creates a tailored application where those tools don’t adequately meet your needs. A project can involve both.

Do we need an audit before a build?

No. You can approach us for any of the three divisions. We’ll still discuss the requirements and technical feasibility before agreeing a project.

How are projects scoped?

We begin with a discovery conversation, then agree the work and a tailored proposal. Software scope includes the intended users, integrations, testing, ownership, hosting and maintenance responsibilities. Ongoing support is optional.

Will you need our account passwords?

We start with the minimum information needed, such as exports, screenshots or workflow discussions. Any system access should be permissioned and proportionate. We never ask for your owner password.
