The challenge

Sales grow. Operational complexity follows.

More orders, channels and customer expectations can expose processes that were never designed for scale. We help teams see what needs attention and where to improve first.

01

Manual work piles up

Repeated spreadsheet updates, disconnected tools and ad-hoc reporting take time away from growing the business.

02

Visibility becomes fragmented

Orders, stock, fulfilment and returns can be difficult to track consistently across channels and teams.

03

Problems repeat

Without clear root-cause analysis, the same customer, inventory and delivery issues can keep coming back.

Questions

What founders usually want to know.

Do you only work with TikTok Shop brands?

No. We work with growing Shopify, TikTok Shop and multichannel ecommerce businesses. TikTok Shop remains one of our specialisms.

What happens during an operations review?

We agree a scope, review relevant workflows and data, then provide a prioritised set of findings and recommendations. Every review is tailored to the business.

Do you need access to our accounts?

We start with minimum necessary information, such as exports or screenshots. Any access should be limited, permissioned and proportionate. We never require your owner password.

Can you help implement improvements?

Potentially, yes. We can discuss a separate, clearly scoped workflow automation or support project once we've understood your needs.
