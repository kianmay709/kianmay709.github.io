# Stonevale Commerce positioning

Agreed direction, 2 November 2026.

Stonevale Commerce is an operations and technology consultancy for growing businesses. Ecommerce, wholesale and product brands are the primary market; other sectors remain welcome when their operational needs fit the work we can realistically deliver.

## Three divisions

1. **Operations Advisory**: audits, workflow reviews, analysis and prioritised recommendations.
2. **Systems & Automation**: configure and connect existing tools, automate repetitive processes and improve information flows.
3. **Custom Software Development**: operational dashboards, wholesale order management, stock and inventory tracking, production planning and bespoke internal tools.

Ongoing support is optional across these divisions. Clients can start with any division; an audit is not required before a build. Prefer a process improvement or existing tool when it meets the need. Scope technical feasibility, access, security, testing, ownership, hosting and maintenance before committing to delivery.

Examples on the homepage are potential solutions, not completed products or case studies. Existing sample audits use fictional data. Do not add unsupported savings claims, clients, testimonials or public prices.

## Outreach approach to retain

- Research the business before writing. Prioritise correct email addresses and phone numbers from official business sources; do not invent missing contact details.
- Begin with a specific, verified observation about the prospect's operation. Distinguish that observation from a possible need: a public email ordering process is evidence of the process, not proof it is inefficient.
- Ask a relevant operational question and introduce the most relevant solution. Do not pitch all three divisions by default or assume every prospect needs an audit.
- Examples: wholesale order intake and production scheduling for a bakery; specifications and production stages for a private-label manufacturer; reporting and stock visibility for a multichannel brand.
- Keep the tone professional, personal and concise, with a brief invitation to talk.
- Keep the website as a clickable link on its own line immediately before the sign-off, separated by a blank line: Kind regards, then Kian May on the next line.
- Retain the existing master prospect sheet and its business name, email and phone columns. Avoid duplicates. Future outreach remains drafts for review unless explicitly authorised for sending.
- An existing introduction attachment should be reviewed for positioning consistency before reuse; do not claim it has been updated as part of this website redesign.

This release does not edit or send existing outreach drafts, alter the prospect sheet, or change the separate acquisition application.
