Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Stonevale Commerce collects and uses personal information when you visit our website, contact us or submit a Profit Leak Audit enquiry.

1. Who we are

Stonevale Commerce is an independent TikTok Shop operations consultancy. For the purposes of UK data protection law, Stonevale Commerce is the controller of personal information described in this policy.

You can contact us at kian@stonevalecommerce.com.

2. Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, work email address, brand name, website or TikTok Shop URL, approximate monthly TikTok Shop sales range, and any information you include in your message.

If you contact us by email, we also receive the information contained in that correspondence. Our hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, assess whether our services are a suitable fit, arrange calls, prepare proposals or audits, provide requested services, maintain appropriate business records, and protect the website from misuse or security issues.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Our lawful bases

Where you ask us about our services, we may process your information because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.

We may also rely on our legitimate interests in operating Stonevale Commerce, responding to business enquiries, maintaining records, improving our processes, and keeping our website and communications secure. We only rely on legitimate interests where those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.

5. Who we share information with

We use a small number of service providers to operate the website and handle enquiries. These currently include:

  • Formspree — processes website enquiry form submissions.
  • Google Workspace — provides business email and related productivity services.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts and delivers this website.

We may also disclose information where required by law, or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

6. International processing

Some of our service providers operate internationally. Where personal information is processed outside the UK, we expect the relevant provider to use appropriate legal safeguards for international transfers where required.

7. How long we keep information

Website enquiry information is generally kept for up to 24 months after our last meaningful contact with you, unless there is a good reason to keep it for longer.

If you become a client, relevant information may be retained for longer where needed to perform our agreement, maintain accounting or business records, or meet legal obligations.

8. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to ask us for access to your personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion or restriction, object to certain processing, or receive certain information in a portable format.

To exercise a privacy right, email kian@stonevalecommerce.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

9. Cookies

Stonevale Commerce does not currently use analytics, advertising or marketing cookies on this website. If that changes, we will update this policy and introduce any consent controls required by law.

10. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we use your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve them.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). You can find more information at ico.org.uk.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, website or data-handling practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page with the date it was last updated.
